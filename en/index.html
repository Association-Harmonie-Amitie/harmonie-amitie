Our mission

Peace, friendship, love — living together in harmony

A non-profit association under the French law of 1901, founded in Paris in 2023, A.H.A. works in three fields: education, culture and solidarity.

From our differences, harmony is born.

News

Latest news

In preparation

Winter collection 2026: donors in China are offering 500 pairs of socks and 24 blanket-capes to the Fleuron Saint-Jean, the Ordre de Malte France's shelter barge for homeless people in Paris. A.H.A. is arranging the shipment.

25 May 2025

At the invitation of the Comité d'animation culturelle Paris 1, organiser of the 15th Festival of Cultural Diversity (17–27 May 2025), held under the patronage of the French National Commission for UNESCO: concert by the Orchestre à cordes de Paris, made up of members of the Orchestre national de France, at the church of Saint-Louis-en-l'Île, organised by A.I.E.C. with support from A.H.A.

25 November 2024

At the initiative of the Association Internationale La Voix de la Compassion, with help from A.H.A.: 40 winter coats donated by a member company in China, transported by a member company in France and handed to the parish priest of a church in the 20th arrondissement of Paris, for people in need.
