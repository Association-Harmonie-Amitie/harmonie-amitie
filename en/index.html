Our mission

What was difficult yesterday becomes possible

A non-profit association under the French law of 1901, founded in Paris in 2023, A.H.A. works in three fields: education, culture and solidarity.

To use the possibilities of the age of artificial intelligence to build connection and trust: supporting young people, caring for our elders, and enabling every person, every community and society as a whole to fulfil, at its own pace, its hopes for harmony and well-being.

Much that was once difficult becomes possible in the age of artificial intelligence.

Our work

Three fields, one commitment to trust

The association is not a placement agency: it acts as an independent third party, and any fee for its services goes into its scholarship fund.

Youth · Education

Training and opening pathways

French-Chinese vocational training with IFAC du Pays de Montbéliard (mandate 2026–2028); further studies and reciprocal student exchanges under discussion with higher-education institutions.

Harmony · Culture

Music, a shared language

Every spring, a concert at the church of Saint-Louis-en-l'Île, as part of the Festival of Cultural Diversity, held under the patronage of the French National Commission for UNESCO.

Elders · Solidarity

Leaving no one alone

Winter collections for care homes, parishes and homeless people; support for the charities of our Honorary Presidents, the Order of Malta and the Association Internationale La Voix de la Compassion.

Governance

Honorary Presidents

Every project of the association is submitted to its two Honorary Presidents for their opinion before it begins.

Mr Charles-Henri d'ARAGON

Honorary Minister Plenipotentiary

Former Ambassador of France to Kuwait, Syria, Saudi Arabia and Luxembourg, then Ambassador of the Sovereign Order of Malta to Lebanon. Officer of the Legion of Honour.

Ms CHOW Ching Ling

Pianist, UNESCO Mozart Medal (2007)

Former professor at the Central Conservatory of Music in Beijing, Officer of Arts and Letters, founder of the Association Internationale La Voix de la Compassion.
